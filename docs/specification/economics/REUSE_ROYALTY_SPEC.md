# Knowledge Reuse Royalty Specification

**Spec ID:** ACS-ECO-006
**Status:** Draft

## Purpose
Reward durable useful knowledge when it prevents repeated fresh inference without creating permanent rent from stale content

## Eligibility
A reuse royalty may be paid only when a requester actually consumes an eligible reusable Memory item under the active search/reuse policy

## Attribution
Policy may split a small reuse charge among original contributor, qualifying verifier(s), knowledge reserve and protocol operations
Exact percentages are versioned policy, not protocol constants

## Freshness
Contradicted, quarantined, stale-beyond-policy or inaccessible Memory cannot silently continue earning normal reuse royalty

## Anti-farming
Self-reuse, same-owner loops, synthetic repeated queries and reciprocal reuse patterns may be excluded or discounted under anti-abuse policy

## Accounting
Every royalty is an ordinary ledger transfer with source Request/search event and policy version

## Conformance
- same logical reuse event pays once
- stale/quarantined item loses normal eligibility
- repeated self-generated cache hits do not mint value
