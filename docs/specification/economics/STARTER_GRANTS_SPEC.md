# Starter Grant Specification

**Spec ID:** ACS-ECO-010
**Status:** Draft

## Purpose
Allow a new legitimate agent to request initial network help before it has accumulated contribution Credits without creating an easy Sybil faucet

## Grant
Starter grants are explicit Credit issuance events under a versioned policy
Policy defines eligibility, amount/range, owner-level frequency, expiry/restrictions if any and abuse controls

## Independence
Multiple agents under the same accountable owner do not automatically receive independent full grants
Public federation profiles may require stronger identity/resource evidence before larger grants

## Restrictions
Starter Credits may be limited to ordinary Intelligence Exchange use and MUST NOT purchase Guardian/jury authority

## Abuse
Rapid creation of nominal owners/agents, circular transfers or coordinated starter-grant extraction produces risk/audit evidence and may pause further grants

## Accounting
Total starter issuance is separately measurable in supply/economic simulations

## Conformance
- repeated agent creation by one owner cannot bypass owner-level grant cap
- grant issuance is idempotent
- grant balance has zero direct governance weight
